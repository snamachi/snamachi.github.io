\section{Strictly Dominant Strategies Imply Unique Nash Equilibrium} 
Suppose each player $i \in [N]$ has a strictly dominant strategy $s^*_i \in S_i$ in the game $G$. Prove $G$ has a unique Nash equilibrium where each player just plays $s^*_i$.



\section{Actions Played in a Nash Equilibrium are Rationalizable}
Suppose  the strategy profile $s^* \in S$ forms a Nash equilibrium for the game $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(u_i)_{i \in [N]})$. Additionally suppose $a_i \in A_i$ is an action such that $s^*_i(a_i)>0$. Prove that $a_i \in A^{\infty}_i$.
