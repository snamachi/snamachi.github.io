\section{Definitions}

An extensive form game is a tuple $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(H_t)_{t \in \mathbb{N}}, (u_i)_{i \in [N]})$.
\\
For each player $i \in [N]$, we have that $A_i$ is a (finite) set of conceivable actions.
\\
As before we write $A$ to denote $\prod_{i \in [N]} A_i$, the set of all conceivable action profiles.
\\
\\
Next, across all times $t \in \mathbb{N}$, we have that each $H_t \subseteq A^t$ is a set of feasible histories of action profiles of length $t$ which satisfy:
\\
1. (Root) $H_0=\{\varepsilon\}$.
\\
2. (Prefix Closure) $\forall a \in A, \ h||a \in H_{t+1} \to h \in H_{t}$
\\
3. (Rectangularity) $\forall a, a', a^* \in A$ such that for each player $i \in [N]$ we have either $a^*_i=a_i$ or $a^*_i=a'_i$ if $h||a \in H_{t+1}$ and $h||a' \in H_{t+1}$ then $h||a^* \in H_{t+1}$.
\\
4. (Finiteness) $\exists T \in \mathbb{N}$ so that $H_T=\varnothing$.
\\
We write $H$ to denote $\bigcup_{t \in \mathbb{N}} H_t$, the set of all feasible histories of action profiles. 
\\
We also write $H_{\bot}=\{h \in H : \forall a \in A, h||a \notin H\}$ to denote the set of terminal histories.
\\
Finally, each agent $i \in [N]$ has a utility function $u_i: H_{\bot} \to \mathbb{R}$.
\\
\\
For each $h \in H$ and $i \in [N]$ we write $A_i(h)=\{a_i \in A_i: \exists a' \in A, a'_i=a_i \text{ and } h||a' \in H\}$ to denote the set of actions available to player $i$ at history $h$.
\\
Next, for each $h \in H$, we write $A(h)$ to denote $\prod_{i \in [N]} A_i(h)$.
\\
An action plan for player $i$ is a function $\pi_i: H\backslash H_{\bot} \to A_i$ such that $\forall h \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$, $\pi_i(h) \in A_i(h)$.
\\
An action plan profile $\pi$ is a tuple $(\pi_i)_{i \in [N]}$ where each $\pi_i$ is an action plan for player $i$.
\\
Note if $\pi$ is an action plan profile, we have $\forall h \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$ that the tuple $(\pi_{i}(h))_{i \in [N]} \in A(h)$.
\\
Given $h \in H_t \backslash H_\bot$, we write $\pi(h) \in H_{t+1}$ to denote the history $h||(\pi_i(h))_{i \in [N]}$.
\\
We also write $h_{\pi} \in H_{\bot}$ to denote the terminal history generated by the action plan profile $\pi$, i.e. $h_{\pi}$ is the final well defined term of the sequence $\varepsilon, \pi(\varepsilon), \pi(\pi(\varepsilon)), \pi(\pi(\pi(\varepsilon)))$...
\\
We take expressions like $u_i(\pi)$ and $u_i(\pi_i;\pi_{-i})$ to simply be shorthand for $u_i(h_{\pi})$.
\\
\\
The normal form representation of an extensive form game $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(H_t)_{t \in \mathbb{N}}, (u_i)_{i \in [N]})$, denoted by NF$(G)=((\Psi^G_i)_{i \in [N]},(v^G_i)_{i \in [N]})$, sets:
\\
 $\Psi^G_i$ to consist of all possible action plans $\pi_i$ for player $i$ in $G$.
 \\
 $\Psi^G$ to consist of all possible action plan profiles $\pi$ in $G$.
 \\
 $v^G_i: \Psi^G \to \mathbb{R}$ to be given by $v^G_i(\pi)=u_i(\pi)$. 
 \\
 We can now port over all our terminology from last week and talk about notions such as best response action plans, one action plan dominating another action plan, rationalizable action plans, mixed action plans, and Nash equilibria in the context of $G$ by simply analyzing NF$(G)$. In particular, we can define a pure Nash equilibrium of $G$ to just be an action plan profile $\pi \in \Psi^G$ such that $\forall i \in [N]$:
 $$\pi_i \in \argmax_{\pi'_i \in \Psi_i^G} u_i(\  \cdot \ ; \pi_{-i})$$
Given an extensive form game $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(H_t)_{t \in \mathbb{N}}, (u_i)_{i \in [N]})$ as well as a nonterminal history $h \in H\backslash H_{\bot}$, we define the subgame $G^{h||}=((A^{h||}_i)_{i \in [N]},(H^{h||}_t)_{t \in \mathbb{N}}, (u^{h||}_i)_{i \in [N]})$\footnote[1]{We use this notation as one can show for all $h' \in H^{h||} \backslash H^{h||}_{\bot}$ that $(G^{h||})^{h'||}=G^{(h||h')||}$.} so that:
\\
$\forall i \in [N], A_i^{h||}=A_i$ and $A^{h||}=A$.
\\
$\forall t \in \mathbb{N}, H^{h||}_t=\{h' \in A^t : h||h' \in H_{t+|h|}\}$.
\\
$u^{h||}_i:H^{h||}_{\bot} \to \mathbb{R}$ is given by $\forall h' \in H^{h||}_\bot$ setting $u^{h||}_i(h')=u_i(h||h')$.
\\
An action plan $\pi_i$ for player $i$ in $G$ naturally induces an action plan $\pi^{h||}_i$ in $G^{h||}$ by $\forall h' \in H^{h||} \backslash H^{h||}_{\bot}$ setting $\pi_i^{h||}(h')=\pi_i(h||h').$ Note this is well defined as $\pi_i(h||h')\in A_i(h||h')=A^{h||}_i(h')$. 
\\
Given an action plan profile $\pi$ in $G$, let $\pi^{h||}$ denote the action plan profile $(\pi^{h||}_i)_{i \in [N]}$ in $G^{h||}$.
\\
A pure subgame perfect equilibrium is an action plan profile $\pi$ in $G$ such that $\forall h \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$ we have that the action plan profile $\pi^{h||}$ is a pure Nash equilibrium for $G^{h||}$, i.e. $\forall i \in [N]$:
$$\pi_i^{h||} \in \argmax_{\pi'_i \in \Psi_i^{G^{h||}}} u_i^{h||}(\ \cdot \ ; \pi^{h||}_{-i}).$$
Given a finite extensive form game $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(H_t)_{t \in \mathbb{N}}, (u_i)_{i \in [N]})$, a strategy plan for player $i$ is a function $\sigma_i: H \backslash H_{\bot} \to \Delta(A_i)$ such that $\forall h  \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$ we actually have $\sigma_i(h) \in \Delta(A_i(h))$.
\\
A strategy plan profile $\sigma$ is a tuple $(\sigma_i)_{i \in [N]}$ where each $\sigma_i$ is a strategy plan for player $i$.
\\
Note $\sigma$ naturally induces a probability distribution over terminal histories $H_{\bot}$ as follows:
\\
Set $\sigma(\varepsilon)=1$. Next, given $h \in H$ and $ a \in A$ such that $h||a \in H$, set: 
$$\sigma(h||a)=\sigma(h) \cdot \prod_{i \in [N]} (\sigma_i(h))(a_i).$$
In particular, $\forall h \in H$ we can interpret $\sigma(h)$ as the probability that the terminal history will begin with $h$ when agents play according to the strategy profile $\sigma$.
\\
Furthermore, we will write $u_i(\sigma)$ as shorthand to mean:
$$u_i(\sigma)=\sum_{h \in H_\bot} u_i(h) \cdot \sigma(h).$$
Let $\chi^G_i$ denote the set of all possible strategy plans $\sigma_i$ for player $i$ in $G$.
\\
Similarly, let $\chi^G$ denote the set of all possible strategy plan profiles in $G$. 
\\
Given $h\in H\backslash H_{\bot}$, a strategy plan $\sigma_i$ for player $i$ in $G$ naturally induces a strategy plan $\sigma_i^{h||}$ in $G^{h||}$ by $\forall h' \in H^{h||} \backslash H^{h||}_{\bot}$ setting $\sigma_i^{h||}(h')=\sigma_i(h||h')$ [valid as $\sigma_i(h||h')\in \Delta(A_i(h||h'))=\Delta((A_i^{h||})(h'))$]. Given a strategy plan profile $\sigma$ in $G$, let $\sigma^{h||}$ denote the strategy plan profile $(\sigma^{h||}_{i})_{i \in[N]}$ in $G^{h||}$. A mixed subgame perfect equilibrium is a strategy plan profile $\sigma$ in $G$ such that $\forall h \in H \backslash H_{\bot},$
$\forall i \in[N]$:
$$\sigma^{h||}_i \in \argmax_{\sigma'_i \in \chi_i^{G^{h||}}} u^{h||}_i( \ \cdot \ ; \sigma_{-i}^{h||}).$$

\section{Examples}
\subsection*{Normal Form Games}
Given a normal form game $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(u_i)_{i \in [N]})$, we can represent it in extensive form by just considering the game EF$(G)=((A_i)_{i \in [N]},(H_{t})_{t \in \mathbb{N}},(u_i)_{i \in [N]})$ where: 
$$H_0=\{\varepsilon\}, H_1=A=\prod_{i \in [N]} A_i, \text{ and } \forall t >1, H_t=\varnothing.$$
Note that $u_i: H_{\bot}\to \mathbb{R}$ is well defined in EF$(G)$ since $H_{\bot}=A$.

\section{Theorems}
\textbf{Theorem 12:} For any finite extensive form game $G$, if for some strategy plan profile $\sigma \in \chi^G$ and player $i \in [N]$ there exists a nonterminal history $h \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$ such that:
$$\sigma^{h||}_i \notin \argmax_{\sigma'_i \in \chi^{G^{h||}}_i} u^{h||}_i(\ \cdot \ ; \sigma_{-i}^{h||})$$
then there exists a strategy plan $\sigma^*_i \in \chi^G_i$ for player $i$  and nonterminal history $h^* \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$ such that $h \sqsubseteq h^*$, $\forall h'\in H \backslash (H_{\bot} \cup \{h^*\})$ we  have $\sigma^*_i(h')=\sigma_i(h')$, and
$u_i^{h^*||}((\sigma^*_i)^{h^*||};  \sigma_{-i}^{h^*||})>u_i^{h^*||}(\sigma^{h^*||}).$ In particular, this means $\sigma^*_i(h^*) \neq \sigma_i(h^*)$. 
\\
\\
\noindent\textbf{Theorem 13:} For any nontrivial finite extensive form game $G$ (i.e. $H \neq \{\varepsilon\}$) there always exists a mixed subgame perfect equilibrium.
\\
\\
\underline{Proof}
\\
Suppose $G=((A_i)_{i \in [N]}, (H_t)_{t \in \mathbb{N}}, (u_i)_{i \in [N]})$ and $T=\min\{t \in \mathbb{N} : H_t=\varnothing\}$ (we know such a $T$ exists by finiteness). By assumption $T>1$. We proceed by induction on $T$. Suppose $T=2$. Note $H_\bot=H_1=\prod_{i \in [N]} A_i(\varepsilon)=A(\varepsilon)$.  Since the only nonterminal history in $H\backslash{H_{\bot}}$ is $\varepsilon$, a strategy plan for player $i$ simply specifies a distribution in $\Delta(A_i(\varepsilon))$ and their utilities are given by a function $u_i: A(\varepsilon) \to \mathbb{R}$. Thus, since the only subgame of $G$ starting at a nonterminal history is $G$ itself, it suffices to show there exists a mixed strategy Nash equilibrium in NF$(G)=((A_i(\varepsilon))_{i \in [N]}, (u_{i})_{i \in [N]})$. But this just immediately follows from Nash's existence result (Theorem 11). 
\\
\\
For the inductive step, assume we have now established the result whenever  $1<T \leq k$. We will next demonstrate the result for  when $T=k+1$. Enumerate the nonterminal histories in $H_1 \backslash H_{\bot}$ by $z_1,...,z_M$ (we can do this because each $A_i$ is finite). Note for each $m \in [M]$ that in the subgame $G^{z_m||}$ we have $1<\min\{t \in \mathbb{N} : H^{z_m||}_t=\varnothing\}\leq k$. Hence, by hypothesis, there exists a strategy plan profile $\zeta^m\in \chi^{G^{z_{m}||}}$ which is a mixed subgame perfect equilibrium in $G^{z_m||}$. 
\\
Consider the normal form game $G'=((A_i(\varepsilon))_{i \in [N]},(u'_i)_{i \in [N]})$ where $u'_i: A({\varepsilon}) \to \mathbb{R}$ is defined by $\forall a \in A({\varepsilon})=H_1$ setting: 
$$u'_i(a)=\begin{cases}u_i(a) & a \in H_{\bot} \\ u^{z_m||}_i(\zeta^m) & a=z_m \end{cases}.$$
Since $G'$ is a normal form game, Nash's existence result (Theorem 11) guarantees that there is a mixed strategy Nash equilibrium $s=(s_i)_{i \in [N]} \in \prod_{i \in [N]} \Delta(A_i(\varepsilon))$ in $G'$. We now define a strategy plan profile $\sigma \in \chi^G$ by for each player $i \in [N]$ setting $\forall h \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$:
$$\sigma_i(h)=\begin{cases}s_i & h=\varepsilon \\
\zeta^m_i(h')&  h=z_m||h' \end{cases}.$$
We claim $\sigma \in \chi^G$ is a mixed subgame perfect equilibrium in $G$. STAC it is not. Then by the one shot deviation principle (Theorem 12), there exists an agent $i \in [N]$, a strategy plan $\sigma^*_i \in \chi^G_i$, and a nonterminal history $h^* \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$ such that $\forall h \in H \backslash (H_\bot \cup \{h^*\})$ we have $\sigma^*_i(h)=\sigma_i(h)$ and:
$$u_i^{h^*||}((\sigma^*_i)^{h^*||};  \sigma_{-i}^{h^*||})>u_i^{h^*||}(\sigma^{h^*||}).$$
It cannot be that there exists some $m \in [M]$ for which $z_m \sqsubseteq h^*$ as otherwise we would have $h^*=z_m||h'$ for some $h' \in H^{z_m||}$ and so:
$${(\zeta^{m}})^{h'||}_i=\sigma_i^{(z_m||h')||} \notin \argmax_{\sigma'_i \in \chi_i^{G^{(z_m||h')||}}} u^{(z_m||h')||}_i(\ \cdot \ ; \sigma_{-i}^{(z_m||h')||}) =\argmax_{\sigma'_i \in \chi_i^{(G^{z_m||})^{h'||}}} ({u_i^{z_m||}})^{h'||}(\ \cdot \ ; (\zeta^m)^{h'||}_{-i}) $$
, i.e. $\zeta^m$ would not be a subgame perfect equilibrium in $G^{z_m||}$. It follows then $h^*=\varepsilon$ as this is the only other nonterminal history and so:
$$u_i(\sigma^*_i;\sigma_{-i})>u_i(\sigma).$$
But by construction we know there exists $s^*_i \in \Delta(A_i(\varepsilon))$ so that $s^*_i \neq s_i$ and  $\forall h \in H \backslash H_{\bot}$:
$$\sigma^*_i(h)=\begin{cases}s^*_i & h=\varepsilon \\ \zeta^m_i(h') & h= z_m||h' \end{cases}.$$
As a result:
\begin{align*}
&u'_i(s^*_i ; s_{-i})=\sum_{a \in A(\varepsilon) : a \in H_\bot}u_i(a) \cdot s^*_i(a_i) \cdot \prod_{j \in [N] \backslash \{i\}} s_j(a_j)+\sum_{m \in [M]}u^{z_m||}_i(\zeta^m) \cdot s^*_i((z_m)_i) \cdot \prod_{j \in [N] \backslash \{i\}} s_j((z_m)_j)
\\
&=\sum_{a \in A(\varepsilon) : a \in H_\bot}u_i(a) \cdot s^*_i(a_i) \cdot \prod_{j \in [N] \backslash \{i\}} s_j(a_j)+\sum_{m \in [M]}u^{z_m||}_i({\sigma^*_i}^{z_m||};{\sigma}^{z_m||}_{-i}) \cdot s^*_i((z_m)_i) \cdot \prod_{j \in [N] \backslash \{i\}} s_j((z_m)_j)
\\
&=\underbrace{u_i(\sigma^*_i; \sigma_{-i})	>u_i(\sigma)}_{\text{key}!}
\\
&=\sum_{a \in A(\varepsilon) : a \in H_\bot}u_i(a)  \cdot \prod_{j \in [N]} s_j(a_j)+\sum_{m \in [M]}u^{z_m||}_i(\sigma^{z_m||}) \cdot  \prod_{j \in [N] } s_j((z_m)_j)
\\
&=\sum_{a \in A(\varepsilon) : a \in H_\bot}u_i(a)  \cdot \prod_{j \in [N]} s_j(a_j)+\sum_{m \in [M]}u^{z_m||}_i(\zeta^m) \cdot  \prod_{j \in [N] } s_j((z_m)_j)=u'_i(s).
\end{align*}
However, this contradicts the fact that $s$ is a Nash equilibrium in $G'$. $\square$
